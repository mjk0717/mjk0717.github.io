1. Overview

L2TP VPN Client ("the App") is developed and published by Guardline Tunnel ("we", "us", "the developer"). The App is a client for connecting to L2TP/IPSec VPN servers that you own or have been authorized to access.

This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information. In summary:

  • The App has no account system. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile.
  • We do not operate any VPN servers. You supply the server yourself.
  • We operate no servers of any kind. The App transmits no data to us, and we hold no user data.
  • Server details you enter are stored encrypted on your device only.
  • Google AdMob is used to display advertising. This is the only path by which any data from the App reaches a third party.

This policy applies to the App only. It does not apply to the VPN servers you choose to connect to, which are operated by you or by third parties.

2. Information we do not collect

Guardline Tunnel never collects, receives, or has access to any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other information that identifies you
  • VPN server addresses, user IDs, passwords, IPSec pre-shared keys, or L2TP tunnel secrets
  • The contents of traffic passing through the VPN tunnel, the websites you visit, or your DNS queries
  • Connection history, session times, or data volumes
  • Contacts, photos, files, call logs, or location data

The App contains no network path to any server operated by us, and includes no analytics, telemetry, or remote logging component. The only outbound connections the App makes are to your chosen VPN server, to Google's advertising services, and to Google Play for billing.

3. Information stored on your device only

When you register a VPN server in the App, the following is saved on your device. None of it is transmitted to us or to any third party.

  • VPN server address
  • User ID and password
  • IPSec pre-shared key (PSK)
  • L2TP tunnel secret, if you configure one
  • Advanced settings such as DNS servers, search domains, and routes

How this data is secured

  • Passwords, IPSec pre-shared keys, and L2TP tunnel secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to storage. They are never written in plain text.
  • The encryption key is generated inside the Android Keystore and never leaves the device. On devices with hardware-backed security, the key remains within the TEE or StrongBox and cannot be extracted even by the App itself.
  • The App declares allowBackup="false", so this data is not carried off the device by cloud backup or device-transfer features.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes this data together with its encryption key, irrecoverably.

4. VPN connections and your traffic

Why the App uses VpnService

Establishing a VPN tunnel is the App's core and only purpose. The App uses Android's VpnService API to create a virtual network interface so that your device's network traffic can be carried, encrypted, to the L2TP/IPSec server you specify. The App does not use VpnService for any other purpose. Android displays a system consent dialog the first time a tunnel is created, and no tunnel can be established without your explicit approval.

How your traffic is handled

  • We do not provide, host, or operate VPN servers. The App is purely a client; you must enter the details of a server you own or are authorized to use.
  • Traffic is forwarded only to the server you specify. We cannot see it, do not log it, and do not share it with anyone.
  • All data between your device and the VPN endpoint is encrypted. Keys are negotiated with IKEv1 (AES-256/AES-128 with SHA-256/SHA-1, Diffie-Hellman group 14 or 2) and the tunnel is protected with IPSec ESP (AES-256/AES-128 with HMAC-SHA-256 or HMAC-SHA-1). This negotiation takes place directly between your device and your server; no system operated by us sits in between.
  • Your traffic is never redirected, inspected, injected into, or monetized. The App does not alter traffic from other apps and does not route it anywhere other than the server you chose.
  • How the operator of the VPN server you choose handles your traffic is outside the scope of this policy. Please review that operator's own policy.

Important — the App's own traffic does not go through the VPN.

When establishing the tunnel, the App excludes itself using addDisallowedApplication. This is technically necessary: without it, the IKE and L2TP control packets that build the tunnel would loop back into the tunnel they are trying to create.

A consequence is that the App's advertising and billing requests leave over your device's ordinary network connection, not the VPN tunnel. Even while the VPN is connected, this traffic does not pass through your VPN server, and Google's advertising services see your real network address rather than the VPN server's. Traffic from every other app on your device is routed through the tunnel as normal.

5. Negotiation log and diagnostic report

So that you can troubleshoot connection problems yourself, the App records the protocol negotiation and displays it on screen.

  • This log is held in memory only while the App is running. It is never transmitted anywhere.
  • It leaves the App only when you press "Copy" or "Share" yourself, and you choose the destination. There is no automatic or background transmission path.

What a diagnostic report contains

SectionContents
AppPackage name, version, build type, install time, install source
DeviceManufacturer, model, Android version, build number, security patch level, ABI
NetworkTransport (Wi-Fi or cellular), interface, MTU, address counts, NAT64 prefix, private DNS status

ResultFailure classification, stage reached, reason, negotiation summary
Layer stateSnapshots of the IKE, L2TP, and PPP state machines

What is excluded from a report

  • Passwords, IPSec pre-shared keys, L2TP tunnel secrets, and user IDs are never included in any form. This is enforced twice over: values are omitted when the report is assembled, and the finished report is then swept with the actual secret strings, replacing any match with ***REDACTED***.
  • Server addresses are truncated to the first two octets (for example, 203.0.x.x).
  • No content of any traffic that passed through the VPN tunnel is included.

Once you export a report, who you send it to is entirely your choice. Please review it before sharing.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and displays advertising through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to cover development and operating costs. This is the only path by which data from the App is shared with a third party.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following:

The party this data is shared with is Google LLC, acting as the advertising provider.

  • All of this data is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).
  • We have no access to this data. The AdMob console shows us only aggregate statistics such as impression counts and revenue. We cannot identify individual users from it.
  • As described in section 4, ad requests do not travel through the VPN tunnel.
  • Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

Controlling or stopping ad data collection

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops ad personalization; non-personalized ads will still be shown. The exact menu location varies by manufacturer and Android version.
  • Purchasing the in-app remove ads product permanently stops all banner and interstitial ads, and with them all data transmission through the advertising SDK.

7. Payments

  • Ad removal is a one-time, non-consumable product (ad_free_forever) sold through Google Play Billing.
  • Payment is processed entirely by Google. We have no access to card numbers, bank details, billing addresses, or any other payment information at any point.
  • The App stores only whether the product is owned — a single true or false value — on your device. This value is never transmitted to us.
  • Refunds and billing enquiries are governed by Google Play's policies.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • We retain no user data whatsoever, because none is ever transmitted to us. There is therefore nothing held by us for you to request access to, correction of, or deletion of.
  • Server entries stored on your device are retained until you delete them. You can remove them individually within the App, or remove all of them together with the encryption key by uninstalling the App. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
  • The negotiation log exists only in memory and is discarded when the App is closed.
  • Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained and processed by Google under its own policies and retention schedules. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings, as described in section 6.
